Flow Forms

Forms that feel like a conversation.

Flow Forms ask one question at a time, with your own work shown beside it. Long surveys stop feeling like paperwork, and people actually reach the end.

  • One question at a time

    A progress bar shows how far along they are, so nobody gives up halfway.

  • Answer with a key press

    Lettered options, pick A, B or C, then Enter. Quick on a laptop, easy on a phone.

  • A gallery of your work

    Upload up to 8 photos. Visitors see them in 3D before the first question, and the thank-you screen keeps them in view.

  • 20 templates, your colors

    Animated backgrounds like Aurora, Midnight and Ocean, which can pick up the colors from your logo.

Standard form or Flow Form? Use a standard form for long, paper-style layouts. Use a Flow Form for surveys, enquiries, bookings and feedback, anything a customer fills in.

Is SARVARC free to use?
Yes, you can start free. The free plan includes basic PDF editing, Make Forms, 3 uses of Refine Report and all the File Converters. For every module, a Day Pass is ₹49 and Workspace is ₹249 a month (beta prices, in INR).
Can I redact my Aadhaar or PAN card online safely?
Yes. SARVARC’s Redact PII tool processes your document entirely in your browser - it is never uploaded to a server. It uses OCR to automatically detect names, addresses, bank details, government ID numbers, and signatures, then lets you black them out before exporting.
Does redacting or blurring actually remove the information?
Solid black or white fill is the only mask style that permanently removes the information. Blur and pixelate can sometimes be reversed with specialist tools, so use solid fill for anything genuinely sensitive.
Can I redact multiple documents at once?
Yes. Upload a batch of scanned IDs or documents and SARVARC groups them by type automatically, so you can review and redact the whole set together instead of one at a time.
Can I move data between tools, like a table into a chart?
Yes. Tables built in Data Arrangement can be pushed directly into Diagrams & Graphs as a chart, and charts, forms, and redacted pages can all be pushed straight onto the Workspace canvas - without exporting and re-uploading between steps.
Why use SARVARC instead of separate tools for each step?
Because the output of one step becomes the input of the next automatically. Most tools force you to export from one app and re-upload into another; SARVARC keeps everything on one connected canvas.
Are my files safe? Do they get uploaded anywhere?
Mostly no. Your files are processed in your browser, and SARVARC File Converter never uploads them. In SARVARC Workspace, your work stays on your device unless you choose to use features that need a server: signing in, Google Drive sync, published forms, team collaboration, or the Kadessa AI assistant. Read our Privacy Policy at sarvarc.com/privacy for details.
Do I need to create an account or sign in?
SARVARC File Converter never requires an account - open any tool and start converting right away. SARVARC Workspace can be explored without an account, and a free account lets you sign in to save sessions and export your finished documents.
Who is SARVARC Workspace for?
SARVARC Workspace is built for freelancers and small businesses who prepare reports, forms, and client documents daily. Refine Report turns messy data into a branded, client-ready report in one click, alongside PDF editing, a spreadsheet, charts, and PII redaction in one connected workspace.
How does Text → Excel work?
SARVARC Workspace auto-detects your delimiter (comma, tab, pipe, or semicolon), identifies what each column contains (numbers, dates, emails, phone numbers, or text), and shows you a live preview before downloading. You can change any column type with one click. No manual formatting needed.
Which browsers are supported?
SARVARC Workspace works best on modern Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera). Firefox and Safari are also supported for most features, though a few advanced capabilities, like animated GIF/WebP frame extraction and video trimming, require Chrome or Edge for full support.
Is there a file size limit?
There is no server-side file size limit because everything runs locally in your browser. Very large files may be slower to process depending on your device’s memory, but there is no hard cap.
Can I use SARVARC Workspace offline?
Yes, for the most part. Once the page and its libraries have loaded, most conversions work without an internet connection, ideal for low-bandwidth or restricted environments.
How do I split a PDF?
Select Split PDF from the sidebar, upload your file, and choose from three modes: split by page ranges (e.g. 1-3, 5-7), split every N pages, or hand-pick individual pages from a visual thumbnail grid. Single-page extractions download as PDF; multi-part splits come as a ZIP.
The conversion failed or the output looks wrong. What do I do?
Try refreshing the page and repeating the conversion. For HTML to PDF, the file is rendered and downloaded automatically with no print dialog. For video trimming, make sure you are using Chrome or Edge. Still stuck? Reach out at contact@sarvarc.com.
What case formats does the Case Converter support?
The Case Converter supports 10 formats: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case (every word capitalised), camelCase (first word lower, rest capitalised, no spaces), PascalCase (every word capitalised, no spaces), snake_case (words joined with underscores), kebab-case (words joined with hyphens), CONSTANT_CASE (snake_case all caps), aLtErNaTiNg (alternates upper and lower on every character), and Inverse Case (flips the existing case of each letter). Just paste your text, pick a format, and copy the result, no file upload needed.
How does the Image Compressor work?
The Image Compressor runs entirely in your browser, no files are uploaded anywhere. Drop in one or multiple JPG, PNG, WebP, or SVG images, set your quality level (1–100), optionally resize by max dimension or target a specific file size, and download all compressed images as a ZIP. You get a live before/after size comparison for every file before downloading.
Can I save my work and continue on another computer?
Yes. SARVARC Workspace lets you download your entire session as a .sw file, a private, self-contained save of your work. Move it to any other device and upload that same .sw file to pick up exactly where you left off, with no cloud account required to carry your progress.
Will more tools be added in the future?
Yes! We are actively expanding SARVARC Workspace with new conversion types and utilities. Our latest additions are the Bulk Image Compressor (compress JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG images in bulk) and the Case Converter. Follow us on LinkedIn and Instagram (@sarvarc_) to stay updated.
