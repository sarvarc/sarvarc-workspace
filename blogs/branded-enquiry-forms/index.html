Branded Enquiry Forms: How to Make Your First Impression Look Like Your Business

November 26, 2026

Most enquiries do not start with a conversation. They start with a message that says "Send details." That is the moment a prospect is most interested, and it is also the moment most businesses lose them: a brochure goes out, three questions come back, and the thread goes quiet.

An enquiry form fixes the process, but a generic one creates a new problem. It looks like it belongs to someone else, so the first real touchpoint with your brand ends up being the least branded thing you own. SARVARC Make Forms was built so the form looks like your business from the first second, and so what comes back is organised instead of scattered across chats. Here is what that means in practice, and why it matters if you run a business. You can read more about our mission on our About Us page.

Key takeaways
  • Brand Kit: save your logo and links once, and every form picks them up.
  • Your own link: a readable name, not a random code.
  • Right questions: six or fewer, mostly choices.
  • Kadessa: describe the form in plain words and she builds it.
  • One inbox: every enquiry sorted, searchable and marked New.

What Is a Branded Enquiry Form?

A branded enquiry form is a form on your own link that collects a customer's details and requirements, and looks like your business while it does it: your logo, your colour, your links. It replaces the back-and-forth of "send details" with one clear step.

The difference between a branded form and a generic one is trust. A visitor who lands on a form with your logo on it and your name in the link knows they are in the right place, and that is a small thing that makes the next step easier to take.

How Does the Brand Kit Put Your Brand on Every Form?

The Brand Kit is a one-time setup that every form can draw from. You save your logo, website and social links once in Settings > Brand Kit, then switch on Make Forms under "Use my Brand Kit in". From that point every form picks up the kit by itself:

  • your logo in the top-left, plus a soft centered watermark
  • your website and every social link you saved
  • an accent colour matched to your logo

There is no button to press and no logo to upload again on each new form. The kit only fills empty spots, so a logo or link you already added is never overwritten. Switch it off and the items the kit added come back out, while anything you added yourself stays.

The same Brand Kit is used in other parts of the Workspace too, such as Workstyle and Slide Templates, so your quotation and your enquiry form carry the same identity.

Can I Get a Clean Link With My Brand Name?

Yes. Instead of a random code, you can type your own link name, so your form lives at an address like this:

forms.sarvarc.com/smart-house-enquiry

Link names use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, from 3 to 40 characters. If someone already uses the name, you are told right away, so two forms never clash. Old links keep working until you change the name, and the app asks you before changing the link of a form that is already live.

A short, readable link is easier to put in a bio, a WhatsApp status, a QR code or a printed flyer, and people are more likely to trust it than a long string of characters.

What Should a Good Enquiry Form Ask?

A good enquiry form asks six questions or fewer. Every extra question costs you enquiries, but too few leaves you chasing details over chat. Here is a set that works for most businesses:

  • Name, plus a phone number or email, so you can reply
  • What they need, as a short choice list (service, product or size)
  • Budget range, as a choice list, so you can tell serious buyers from browsers
  • Location or site
  • Best time to contact them
  • One open box: "Anything else we should know?"

None of these questions are hard to write. The skill is in keeping the list short. Use choice questions for most answers and keep typing for the name and the open box. People finish choices faster, and your replies are easier to sort later. Make the contact detail required and the rest optional.

Where Do My Enquiries Go?

Every enquiry lands in the Fill & Responses tab of Make Forms, and a bell icon in the Workspace shows new ones as they arrive. Each response is its own card with:

  • the person's name and email
  • a badge for where it came from (online, entered by you, or from a table)
  • a badge showing how complete it is, for example "3 of 5 answered"
  • a New tag until you open it

A summary strip shows Total, New and Today, and filter chips plus a search across every answer help you find one enquiry among many. Opening a response shows an answer sheet with each question and its reply, and required questions left blank are marked.

One detail matters for enquiries. When a buyer types a budget like "₹1 crore to ₹2 crore", it reaches your table exactly as typed, not rounded or cut down to one figure.

How Do I Avoid Fake or Spam Enquiries?

Every submission goes through a Cloudflare Turnstile check before it is saved, and the answers are checked for size and valid fields. You can also require name and email, so you never receive an enquiry you cannot reply to.

Set Up Your Branded Enquiry Form in 6 Steps

  1. Open Settings > Brand Kit and save your logo, website and social links.
  2. Under "Use my Brand Kit in", switch on Make Forms.
  3. Open Make Forms and start a new form. Add your six questions.
  4. In the Details tab, type your link name, like your-brand-enquiry.
  5. Press Update Link to publish.
  6. Share your short link on Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and your website.

A Practical Example

Say you run a solar installation business and most of your enquiries arrive through Instagram and WhatsApp. You save your logo and Instagram link in the Brand Kit once. You build a form with six questions: name and phone, rooftop or ground-mounted, monthly electricity bill range, city, best time to call, and an open box. You name the link and put it in your Instagram bio.

From then on, a visitor opens a form that carries your logo and your colour, answers in under a minute, and the enquiry appears in your Workspace with a New tag. You open it, read the answers, and call at the time they chose. No "send details", no brochure sent blind, no thread to chase.

Who Is a Branded Enquiry Form For?

Anyone who gets enquiries from social media or word of mouth: solar and construction companies, interior designers, real estate agents, clinics, coaching classes, skincare and fashion brands, agencies and freelancers. If a customer's first move is to message you and ask, a branded enquiry form turns that message into a structured step.
