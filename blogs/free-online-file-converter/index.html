Are My Files Uploaded When I Use These Tools?

For file conversions, no. The tools do their work in your browser, so your PDF, photo or video stays on your device. That matters for the files people convert most often, such as ID photos, signed forms, bank statements and contracts, which you would rather not hand to an unknown server.

One mode works differently, and it is worth knowing about. HTML to PDF can take a web address, and to fetch a page you do not own it sends that address to outside services. If the page is private or sensitive, use the other two modes in the same tool: upload the HTML file, or paste the HTML.

Because your own device does the work, very large files take longer and depend on how much memory your phone or laptop has. The tools set no size cap of their own.

How Do I Handle the Most Common Jobs?

The real saving comes from using two or three tools in a row. These are the situations that come up most often.

You need to submit documents as one PDF

This is the classic form, admission or application upload: a few photos of documents, and a portal that accepts one PDF.

  1. Image to PDF: combine the photos and put them in the right order.
  2. Merge PDF: add any documents you already have as PDFs, such as a resume.
  3. Compress PDF: if the portal has a size limit, compress the result. Photos of documents are exactly the kind of image-heavy file this tool shrinks well.

You only need a few pages of a long PDF

Use Split PDF in Pick Pages mode, tap the thumbnails you want, and download them as a new PDF. The same mode removes pages: keep the ones you want and leave the rest behind.

You need to send a Word document as a PDF

Convert the .docx with Word to PDF so the layout cannot shift on someone else's screen. If the result is too large to email, run it through Compress PDF afterwards.

Your website or email images are too heavy

Run them through the Image Compressor first. If you also want a different format, such as WebP for a website, switch it with the Image Format Converter. Use PNG for logos and anything that needs transparency, and JPEG for photos where size matters more than perfection.

You need a thumbnail or a screenshot from a video

Open the video in Video Frame Capture, scrub to the exact moment, and capture it as JPG or PNG. For a set of frames, turn on burst mode, choose an interval such as every second, and download them all as a ZIP.

You are moving data between tools

Convert a CSV into JSON for a developer, or a JSON export into a spreadsheet for a colleague. Turn a CSV into an Excel file, or export a sheet from Excel as CSV. For naming, the Case Converter turns a plain phrase into camelCase, snake_case or kebab-case in one click.

What Do the Free Converters Not Do (Yet)?

Being clear about limits saves you time, so here they are.

  • Compress PDF only touches images. It recompresses the JPEG images inside a PDF. Text and vector graphics are never changed, so a text-only PDF will not get smaller. Image-heavy scans often shrink noticeably, while PDFs with already-optimised images shrink less.
  • Word to PDF takes .docx only. Older .doc files need to be saved as .docx first.
  • Image to PDF works directly with JPG and PNG. For anything else, change the format first.
  • Video tools depend on your browser. Frame capture works with formats your browser can play, such as MP4, WebM and OGG. Other formats may not open, depending on your device.
  • Animated GIF and WebP frames are fully extracted in Chromium-based browsers such as Chrome and Edge. Other browsers may export only the first frame.
